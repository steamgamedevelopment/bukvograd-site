About the game

Which ages is Bukvograd for?

Children aged 3 to 7. Every child travels the same story, but the games, targets and pace adapt: a three-year-old who can’t read yet hears every instruction and answers with pictures, while a six-year-old reads words and sentences and writes letters from memory.

What language is the game in?

The game itself is in Macedonian: the voices, the letters, the words and the stories. The parent area is in Macedonian, English and German, and every Macedonian word shown to parents has a Latin transliteration, a translation and a listen button.

Is it suitable for children growing up abroad?

Yes. Besides letters and reading, children practise listening and speaking with everyday phrases, and you can switch on slower speech. In your child’s profile you can note that you live outside North Macedonia. The parent area suggests simple ways to practise at home – even if you don’t read Cyrillic yourself.

Does it follow what children learn at school?

It follows the Grade 1 textbook used in North Macedonia: children start with the same 15 letters as the textbook, capital and small letters are learned together, and every letter is written with the school’s shapes and stroke order. If your child is already in first grade, you can switch to the textbook’s exact letter order in the parent area.

Bukvograd is an independent product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education and Science.

How long is a session? Will my child want to play for hours?

Sessions are short – about 5 to 15 minutes, depending on age – and every session ends with a calm “time to rest” scene. In the parent area you can set a daily limit and quiet hours; when time is up, the same calm scene appears and only a grown-up can extend it.

There are no streaks, countdowns or notifications, and no character ever makes a child feel bad for stopping.

What happens when the story is finished?

The village stays. Every day Riso plans a short walk – a little review, one new thing, a step towards school – and the toys in the playground and Quick Geography (Брза географија) never run out, because each one adapts to your child. The village follows the seasons and the holidays you choose, new mini-stories arrive with updates, and on each birthday the games move up a step. Your child can keep playing from age 3 until school starts, and beyond.

What is Quiet time?

For the moments when you need a few minutes – cooking, a phone call, a waiting room. In the parent area, choose Quiet time, a length (10 to 30 minutes), with or without sound, and Riso’s walk or the playground. Your child plays learning games only; when the time is up, the game ends calmly and rests until you open it again. Afterwards you see what your child did.

How do I keep my child inside the game?

On iPhone and iPad, turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access). With the game open, triple-click the side button (or the Home button) and tap Start: your child can’t leave the app until you end it with the same triple-click and your passcode. On Android, use app pinning (Settings → Security → App pinning).

What is there for left-handed children and for accessibility?

Left-handed mode mirrors the writing models and panels. There are also subtitles (on by default), slower speech, reduced motion, a colour-blind-safe palette in which shapes and patterns never rely on colour alone, and separate volumes for music, voice and sound effects.

Children only tap, drag and trace – no fast gestures and no typing – and every button is large.

Which devices does it work on? Does it need the internet?

iPhone and iPad (iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later) and Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or later). The app needs about 200 MB of free space.

You need the internet to download the app and to subscribe. After that, everything works offline; the app checks the subscription with the store whenever the device happens to be online.

Subscription and payments

What is free?

Chapter 1 is free forever: the village of Bukvograd and the meadow, with the letters А, М, Т, О and Е, numbers to 5, the first word (мама) and the first sentence, and three toys in the playground – plus the whole parent area. There is no time limit, and it is not a trial.

What does the subscription include, and what does it cost?

One subscription – monthly or yearly – unlocks the whole journey and the years after it: all eight lands, the finale, Riso’s daily walks, the whole playground, Quick Geography and the new stories and content we add while you are subscribed – for every child on the device (up to six profiles).

It costs around €2.99 a month or €24.99 a year; the store shows the exact price in your currency before you confirm. There is no trial that turns into a payment: Chapter 1 is free instead, and the subscription simply renews each month or each year. You can cancel at any time.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Буквоград and pick the other plan; the App Store tells you when the change takes effect. On Android, cancel the current plan in Google Play and, when it ends, choose the other one in the parent area.

How do I subscribe?

Tap the grown-up button in the top-right corner of the game, answer the short question for adults and open Subscription. Your store then asks you to confirm. Children never see prices or purchase buttons.

Can we use it on more than one device? What about a new phone or tablet?
  • iPhone and iPad: the subscription works on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Android: it works on devices signed in with the Google account that bought it.
  • A subscription bought on the App Store doesn’t work on Android, and the other way round.
  • Progress is saved on each device separately, because we don’t use accounts or cloud servers. It moves to a new device only through your device’s own backup or transfer.
  • After reinstalling, or on a new device, the subscription normally comes back by itself. If it doesn’t, open the parent area → Subscription → Restore purchases.
How do I cancel?
  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Буквоград and tap Cancel Subscription (Apple’s guide).
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Буквоград and tap Cancel subscription (Google’s guide).

You can also get there from the parent area (Subscription → Manage or cancel subscription). Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After you cancel, the full game stays open until the end of the period you have paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them – we never see your payment details and cannot refund you ourselves.

  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: see Google Play’s refund policies, or open your order history in Google Play.

If you live in the EU or the UK, the store also handles your legal right of withdrawal. If something doesn’t work, please write to us – we’d like to help either way.

What happens if a payment fails, or if I stop subscribing?

If a renewal payment fails, the store tries again, and during its billing grace period (up to 3 days) the full game stays open. After that, everything except Chapter 1 is locked – nothing is deleted.

Profiles, progress, letters and stickers all stay, and as soon as the subscription is active again, everything opens where your child left off. Your child sees a friendly scene in which Riso asks a grown-up for help – never a price.

My card was declined. What can I do?

Check that your card is enabled for online payments, or add another payment method to your store account. In North Macedonia, both stores accept payment cards and charge in US dollars; ask your bank to allow online and international payments for your card. On iPhone you can also use Apple Pay if your bank supports it. Google Play doesn’t accept virtual cards.

Privacy and safety

What data do you collect about my child?

None. Profiles, progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat. The details are in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my child’s data?

In the parent area, open Children, choose the child and tap Delete this profile – this removes the profile and all of its progress from the device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app saved on that device. If your device makes backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), you manage those copies in your device settings.

What is the grown-up check?

A short question for adults, written out in words – for example a multiplication – that you answer on a number pad. A new question appears every time, and the voice never helps. It protects the subscription, the settings, deleting profiles and every link out of the app, and the parent area locks again when you leave it.

It keeps small children from buying things or leaving the app by accident. It does not collect anything.

Why might my child’s device ask a parent before using an update?

In some places, such as the US states of Texas, Louisiana and Utah, the law asks apps to confirm a user’s age category through the App Store and to get a parent’s approval before a child uses an update that changes the app significantly. If that ever applies to an update of Bukvograd, Riso asks your child to fetch a grown-up, and Apple sends you a request to approve. Everyday updates – new chapters, games or fixes – never ask. The age information stays on the device and never reaches us.

Does the app use the microphone?

Only if you switch on the optional speaking game in the parent area – it is off by default. Your child’s voice is then held in memory for a few seconds so they can hear themselves, and then discarded. Nothing is recorded permanently, saved or sent. Everything else works without the microphone.
