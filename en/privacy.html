In short

  • The Bukvograd app does not collect personal data. Nothing your child does in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Profiles, progress and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no chat, no location, no camera.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to help you.

For children

Everything you do in Буквоград stays on this tablet or phone. Riso keeps your letters and stickers safe at home. Nobody else can see them – not even the people who made the game. If something needs changing, ask a grown-up to help.

2. What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else – the app has no server of ours to talk to.

WhatWhy
Child profiles: the first name or nickname you type in, the chosen hero (Marko or Ana), a picture emblem, the child’s age (year of birth – and the month, if you add it – or age group), writing hand, whether you live outside North Macedonia, the school year (when first grade starts and where the class is) and, if you choose them, the sounds and steps for speech practiceTo greet your child, use the right boy or girl forms, pick a starting level, adapt writing for left-handed children, let the games grow on each birthday, keep pace with the class and practise the sounds you chose
Learning progress: which letters, words and numbers have been practised, answers in activities, time played, stickers and other rewardsSo the game can teach the next step, repeat things at the right time and show you progress
Settings: parent-area language, volumes, subtitles, daily limit, quiet hours, Quiet time, the holidays you choose and similar choicesTo remember your choices
Subscription status: a copy of the purchase information that Apple or Google give the app on your deviceSo the full game opens even without internet
A small time record (no identifiers)So the subscription lock works offline and cannot be fooled by changing the device clock

The app also reads your device’s language and region settings: the language to choose the language of the parent area, and the region (never your location) so that the village shows the right season – south of the equator, the seasons are turned round. This stays on the device. Keeping this information on your device is strictly necessary for the game you asked for, so no consent is needed for it (EU ePrivacy Directive, Art. 5(3)).

Deleting it: in the parent area, open Children, choose the child and tap Delete this profile; this removes the profile and all of its progress. Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app saved on that device (see section 6 for backups).

3. What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect personal data through the app.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting services and no third-party SDKs for analytics or advertising.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no social features.
  • We don’t use advertising identifiers or any other identifier to recognise a device or a person.
  • No location, camera, contacts or photos.
  • No online AI services: nothing your child says or does is analysed outside the device.
  • We don’t sell or share data. There is none to sell or share.

4. When the app goes online

The app works fully offline. When the device is online, the app talks only to the store’s own purchase service on your device – Apple’s App Store or Google Play – to check the subscription, to buy it if you choose to, or to restore purchases. App updates come through the store. Links in the parent area, such as the link to this policy, open in your browser only after the grown-ups’ check.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

We don’t receive your payment details, your name or your email address from these purchases. The stores send us sales and payout reports (for example, how many subscriptions were sold in which country), which we use only for accounting and tax. If a report or a message from you includes an order number, we use it only to help you or to meet legal obligations.

If you have agreed in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may also send us crash reports and anonymous usage statistics. These come from the operating system, not from the app, and do not identify you or your child; we use them only to fix problems.

Age checks that some laws require

In some places, such as the US states of Texas, Louisiana and Utah, the law asks apps to confirm a user’s age category through the App Store, and to ask a parent before a minor uses an update that changes the app significantly. On an iPhone or iPad in such a place, the app asks the App Store for the age category (for example “under 13”) and, when an update needs it, sends a request to a parent through Apple. This happens on the device: the app uses the answer only for that step, does not store it and never sends it to us or anyone else.

6. Device backups and new devices

Your device may include the app’s data in its own backups – for example iCloud Backup, a backup on your computer, or an Android backup to your Google account. These backups belong to you and are kept by Apple or Google under their terms; we cannot access them. Restoring such a backup, or moving to a new device with your device’s transfer tool, can bring your child’s progress along. If you don’t want the app’s data in your backups, change the backup settings on your device.

7. Speaking practice and the microphone

The app includes an optional speaking game, “Папагалче” (Little parrot): your child says a word and hears their own voice straight away. It is off by default, and only a grown-up can turn it on in the parent area; your device then asks for permission to use the microphone. While the app is listening, the parrot glows and your device shows its own microphone indicator. The voice is held in the device’s memory for a few seconds to play it back and is then discarded. It is never saved, never sent anywhere and never analysed to recognise speech or to identify anyone. In the breathing games (“Дувај!”) the app only measures how hard your child is blowing, moment by moment, to move the picture, and in First words (“Први зборчиња”) only whether your child is making a sound; no sound is kept at all, not even in memory. You can turn the feature off in the parent area, or remove the permission in your device settings, at any time. Everything else in the game works without it.

8. The grown-ups’ check

Purchases, the subscription screen, settings that change data or capabilities (such as deleting a profile or turning on the microphone) and every link out of the app are in the parent area, behind a short task for grown-ups – a question written out in words. It stops young children from buying things or leaving the app by accident. It is a safety lock, not a way of collecting consent, and it records nothing.

10. Children’s privacy

Bukvograd is designed for children aged 3 to 7. A parent or guardian sets it up and decides on any purchase.

  • United States (COPPA): we do not collect personal information from children. Everything the app saves stays on the device and is never transmitted to us or to third parties. If you believe we have received information about a child – for example in an email – contact us and we will delete it.
  • EU and UK GDPR, North Macedonia: because the app does not process children’s data on our side, we do not ask anyone for consent. The data on the device stays under your control. We follow data protection by design and by default: the app keeps only what the game needs, the most private settings are the defaults (for example, the microphone is off), and you can delete everything at any time. We use the UK Age Appropriate Design Code as a guide even where it does not formally apply.
  • We never use children’s data for advertising, profiling or marketing.

11. How long information is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete the profile or uninstall the app.
  • Support emails: 12 months after your request is closed, then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Store reports: as long as accounting and tax law requires (up to 10 years).
  • Website logs: we keep none. Our hosting provider keeps its server logs for security, as its own privacy statement describes.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority – for example the Agency for Personal Data Protection of North Macedonia (azlp.mk) or the data protection authority where you live (list of EU authorities; in the UK, the ICO).

For data on your device, you can use these rights directly: view and change it in the parent area, or delete it. For anything we hold, such as emails, write to veljanoskimihail@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

13. Security

App data stays in the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by your device’s own security, such as the passcode and device encryption. We recommend using a passcode and keeping the system up to date. Support emails are kept in a protected mailbox that only authorised people can open.

14. International transfers

The app transfers no data. This website is hosted by GitHub in the USA, and support emails may be stored by Google (Gmail) in the USA and other countries. Where information leaves North Macedonia or the EU, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how the app handles data, we will explain it in the app’s update notes and in the parent area before it takes effect.
