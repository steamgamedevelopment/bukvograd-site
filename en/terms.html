6. Payment problems

If a renewal payment fails, the store will try again. During the store’s billing grace period (currently up to 3 days), the full game stays open. After that, everything except Chapter 1 is locked until the payment goes through or you subscribe again. When the subscription is active again, the content unlocks by itself.

7. When a subscription ends

If you cancel, or a payment cannot be collected:

  • Chapter 1 stays open.
  • All profiles, progress, letters learned and stickers stay on the device.
  • Locked chapters show a friendly scene in which Riso asks a grown-up for help. Your child never sees prices or purchase buttons.
  • If you subscribe again later, everything opens where your child left off.

8. Using the app offline

The app works without internet. It checks the subscription with the store whenever the device is online. If the device stays offline after the end of a billing period, the app keeps the paid chapters open for up to 3 days and then waits until it can confirm the renewal. Changing the device’s date or time does not extend access.
